# Icon creation

Almost never draw one. `ICO-1` makes `lucide-react` the only icon dependency registry sources may
import, and 122 of its glyphs are already in use here, so a mark that does not exist yet is usually
a mark that should be a different lucide glyph. This page is about the small remaining case: a
drawing whose geometry carries information, which lucide cannot supply because the information is
not known until render. Choosing between existing glyphs, and sizing them, is
**Foundations → Iconography**.

## What a custom drawing has to sit beside

The constraints are the installed package's, not a house invention. lucide-react 1.17.0 renders
every glyph from one `defaultAttributes` object: a `0 0 24 24` viewBox, `fill: "none"`,
`stroke: "currentColor"`, `strokeWidth: 2`, and `strokeLinecap` and `strokeLinejoin` both
`"round"`. A 24-unit grid, an unfilled 2-unit stroke, round ends.

Two of those are load-bearing beyond appearance. `stroke: "currentColor"` is why an icon is its
label's colour by construction rather than by discipline, and `fill: "none"` is why a glyph never
carries a second colour decision. Match both, whatever grid you draw on.

## Three drawings ship, and none of them is a pictogram

Counted across the 144 non-test registry sources on 2026-09-09, three files contain a hand-written
`<svg>`, and none of them is a pictogram.

- `super-ai/credits-indicator.tsx` line 131, a progress ring. Two concentric circles on a
  `0 0 16 16` grid at `strokeWidth` 2.5: a track painted `stroke-border`, and an arc painted
  `stroke="currentColor"` whose `strokeDasharray` and `strokeDashoffset` are computed from the
  balance. The arc is the number. It is rendered `-rotate-90` so the arc starts at twelve o'clock,
  sized `size-3.5` to sit on the same rung as a lucide glyph in the same pill, and marked
  `aria-hidden` because the balance beside it is already the announcement.
- `marketing/dot-pattern.tsx` line 28 and `marketing/orbiting-circles.tsx` line 44. Both are
  surfaces rather than glyphs, a dot field and an orbit path, both `aria-hidden="true"`, and both
  sized to their container rather than to an icon rung.

Three more live in the vendored AI Elements ports this repo displays but does not author:
`ai-elements/loader.tsx`, whose ten spokes step opacity from 1 down to 0.1 so a rotation reads as
direction, plus `ai-elements/context.tsx` and `ai-elements/open-in-chat.tsx`.

## The test the case has to pass

Ask what the drawing knows that a file cannot. A ring whose sweep is the quota, or a spinner whose
spoke opacity is the direction of travel: the geometry is a function of the data, and no fixed
asset can stand in. That is the whole sanctioned category.

Neither gate will stop you getting this wrong. `ICO-1` inspects import specifiers, so an inline
`<svg>` is invisible to it, and `ICO-2` greps for emoji. The decision is read-work at review.

## Before it ships

- **Stroke and fill.** `stroke="currentColor"` or a semantic stroke utility such as
  `stroke-border`; `fill="none"` unless the mark is genuinely solid. No hex and no `oklch()`, which
  `TOK-1`, `TOK-2` and `TOK-7` fail as blockers. No opacity composite on a `stroke-*` or `fill-*`
  outside the pinned steps, which `TOK-8` fails.
- **Size.** Render it at the rungs the registry actually uses, 12, 14 and 16 pixels, and check the
  stroke still reads at the smallest. Weight is a decision your grid forces: `credits-indicator`
  draws at `strokeWidth` 2.5 on a 16-unit grid at 14 pixels, close to twice the effective line a
  lucide glyph puts down at the same rung.
- **Both themes.** Toggle the Theme control. A drawing that resolves through `currentColor` and
  semantic stroke utilities follows the theme for free; a literal does not.
- **Name or hide it.** `aria-hidden` when something beside it already says the number, a
  `role="img"` and a name when it is the only announcement.
- **A story.** A new drawing arrives inside a component, and that component's story is what puts
  it in front of axe. Nothing audits a glyph on its own.

## Rules

- Reach for a different lucide glyph first. 122 are already in use, and `ICO-1` makes lucide the
  only importable set.
- Draw only when the geometry carries data or motion no fixed asset can hold. A missing concept
  mark is not that case.
- Match lucide's contract: unfilled, `currentColor` stroke, round caps and joins, and a stroke
  weight that still reads at 12 pixels.
- Never put a raw colour in the drawing. `TOK-1`, `TOK-2` and `TOK-7` are blockers, and `TOK-8`
  bans a foreground composite on `stroke-*` and `fill-*` alike.
- Mark it `aria-hidden` when the value is already announced beside it, and name it when it is not.
- Neither icon rule can see an inline `<svg>`, so this decision is made at review and recorded in
  the component.
