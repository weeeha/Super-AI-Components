# Principles

Ten positions this repo has already taken, each one written down somewhere under `docs/` and most
of them enforced by something that runs in CI. They are here because a reader composing these
components into an application will hit all ten, and because a page that restates a contract goes
stale while a page that cites one does not.

## 1. The registry is the product

What ships is a shadcn registry a consumer installs item by item, and the source lands in their
app. `README.md` records the catalog as 116 of 116 items shipped, plus a second namespace of 15
marketing items. The workbench you are reading exists to show that output; it is not the artifact.

Two consequences a consumer feels directly. Upstream changes reach new installs only, never a
running app, which is what makes composing against a fast-moving upstream safe
(`docs/design-system/concept-model.md` §1). And every decision below has to survive being copied
into a codebase nobody here controls.

## 2. Primitives, contracts, automated checks

The chosen consistency approach, named in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-10-super-ai-components-design.md`
§6: consumers copy readable code, and consistency comes from structure and CI rather than from
abstraction. There is no wrapper layer holding the system together.

Seven contracts cross the families (`docs/design-system/concept-model.md` §4). The Cost contract is
the shortest to show: one number in three forms, a chip per action, a line before commit, a ring at
app level. The chip is `cost-chip`.

## 3. A component earns its place in three unrelated products

Decision D1, `docs/design-system/decisions.md`. Registry status requires the pattern to appear in
three or more unrelated products on the reference board. One product makes it a demo. This is the
test that holds the catalog at its current size while whole families were added.

## 4. Compose before you build, and promote upward

Decision D3, same file. L3 components never import each other. If two of them need the same piece,
the piece moves up to L2 rather than sideways. The rule produced five primitives that had been
hiding inside leaf components.

The layer order is L0 shadcn primitives, L1 AI Elements, L2 the Super AI primitives, L3 the
components, L4 the blocks (`concept-model.md` §1). Depend downward only.

## 5. Every story this repo owns is an accessibility test

`pnpm test:stories` runs axe over the stories in `stories/super-ai/**` and `stories/marketing/**`
in real Chromium, and fails CI. Vendored ports are excluded by directory and nothing this repo
authored is excluded by name. The full audit that decided this is
`docs/design-system/a11y-baseline.md` §What the gate enforces today.

## 6. Guidance ships with the component

Each item carries a `<name>.docs.tsx` module with required fields, including at least two dos and
two donts with live examples, and an `accessibility` block whose `keyboard` and `screenReader` arms
are both checked. `docs/design-system/component-build-brief.md` §Guidance is the contract, and
`apps/docs/scripts/check-citations.mts` is the gate. A component without its guidance is not done.

## 7. One gate list, and the CI file is the copy that counts

`.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs, in order: `pnpm lint`, `format:check`, `typecheck`,
`check:tokens`, `check:contract`, `test`, `build:registry`, `build`, a Playwright smoke, the
Storybook a11y and interaction run, and the consumer install test
(`apps/docs/scripts/consumer-test.sh`). Read the order there rather than from any prose copy of it,
this page included.

## 8. Exclusion lists only shrink

Stated at `a11y-baseline.md` line 117 and paired across two files by `check:contract`. Measured
2026-09-09, both lists are empty of this repo's own work: `CONTRAST_EXEMPT_FILES` in
`packages/ds-rules/src/token-rules.mjs` line 15 is `[]`, and the exclusion block in
`apps/storybook/vitest.config.ts` names only the vendored `stories/ui/**` and
`stories/ai-elements/**` directories plus three mount-crash files inside them. Adding a name to
either list to quiet a new failure is the move the rule exists to stop.

## 9. A component sizes itself against its own width

Decision D19. Columns key off the component's own width, never the viewport, because a grid placed
beside a sidebar is a container problem and no viewport value can fix it. Measured 2026-09-09, two
registry sources have converted: `artifact-grid.tsx` and `recent-grid.tsx`. The shape and the two
thresholds are on **Foundations → Layout & spacing**.

## 10. Strings ship in English, and that is a recorded decision

Decision D22, dated 2026-09-07. 29 registry components hardcode `aria-label` text and 6 hardcode
placeholders, and no component invents a labels prop. The reasoning is asymmetry: a later additive
prop is cheap, and a prop shape published to every consumer cannot be walked back. It is revisited
when a consumer asks or a second language ships.

## Rules

- Cite the contract, do not copy it. Every claim above names a file; the file is what changes.
- A new component ships with its story and its docs module in the same change.
- Do not add a name to an exclusion list. Fix the component, or record the decision where the list
  is defined.
- Read `.github/workflows/ci.yml` for the gate order before trusting any prose about it.
- Token questions go to **Foundations → Design Tokens**; the contrast pairing that keeps failing is
  on **Foundations → Color**.
