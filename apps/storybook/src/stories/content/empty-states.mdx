# Empty states

A region with nothing in it is the one moment where every word has to carry. Three different things
empty a region and they want three different scripts: **nothing yet**, **nothing matched**,
**nothing loaded**. A generic "Nothing here" answers none of them, and the registry ships zero of
those. Everything below was measured on 2026-09-09 across the 129 component files in
`apps/docs/registry/super-ai/`.

## One component, three sizes

<Example>
  <EmptyStateDemo />
</Example>

`empty-state.tsx` takes `size` as a prop rather than forking, so `page`, `panel` and `in-grid` emit
the same slots in the same order and differ only in the frame (`SIZE_ROOT`, lines 49 to 57). The
`in-grid` tile fills its cell and keeps the grid's columns, which is what stops an empty result set
turning a grid into a page takeover.

Fifteen registry files import it and 31 instances render across them, so a shell almost never
writes its own empty region. Blocks have to prove they thought about this: an `Empty` story export
is one of the four assertions the block branch of `apps/docs/scripts/check-contract.mts` makes, per
`docs/design-system/block-build-brief.md`. Twenty-two story files under
`apps/storybook/src/stories/super-ai/` export one today.

Two API decisions are copy decisions in disguise. There is no `ctaLabel` prop, recorded at lines 26
to 31, so nobody can reach for a generic label by default; the `action` is caller-supplied because
the CTA has to repeat the verb of the surface the empty state is standing in for. And there is no
illustration slot at all: the decoration on offer is one lucide glyph, rendered `aria-hidden`.

## Nothing yet

The title names what the region holds. The body says how the first one comes to exist. The action
creates it, when creating is one control away.

The shipped titles follow that to the letter, and they are short: "Nothing pinned yet"
(`home-shell.tsx:260`), "No records yet" (`records-shell.tsx:453`), "Nothing on the canvas yet"
(`studio-shell.tsx:400`), "No sources yet" (`source-panel.tsx:265`). The body underneath does the
teaching. Most run to one sentence; five `description` props in the whole tree run to two, among
them `records-shell.tsx:454`, which uses the second sentence to say what a record is before saying
what appears once you make one. None runs to three, so two is the ceiling.

Descriptions that describe the mechanism beat descriptions that describe the feeling.
`source-panel.tsx:266` explains what happens to a document after it is added, which is the fact a
first-time reader is missing.

## Nothing matched

A filter emptied the region, so the copy names the filter as the cause and the action undoes it.

`library-shell.tsx` lines 336 to 343 is the cleanest worked example in the repo: one `EmptyState`,
two titles and two descriptions, switched on a `narrowed` flag computed one line above from the
facets, the search string, and the active saved search. Filtered, it reads "No matches" over "Clear
a filter in the rail to widen the search." Unfiltered, it reads "Nothing saved yet" over a sentence
about what gets archived there. `artifact-shell.tsx` lines 276 to 282 does the same for its result
summary and again at line 464 for the region itself.

Two details from `artifact-shell` are worth copying. Its filtered empty carries `role="status"`
(line 463) with a comment saying that a silent disappearance is indistinguishable from a broken
filter. And the summary line says the count, "N of M artifacts", which is the thing that separates a
filtered list from an empty workspace.

## Nothing loaded

A request that failed leaves an empty region too, and this is the species the registry has never
shipped at collection scale. No `EmptyState` instance in the tree says a list failed to load, and
none of them offers a retry. The only load failure with words on it is per item:
`reference-strip.tsx:114` renders "Couldn't load" in the failed tile's action slot.

That makes this a rule going forward. When one arrives, the words are
**Content → Error messages**' formula sitting inside an empty state's frame: what happened in plain
past tense, the cause when it changes the remedy, then a verb. A filtered view whose request fails
belongs here too, because the filter had nothing to do with why the region is empty.

<Example>
  <EmptyState
    size="panel"
    title="Renders didn't load"
    description="The request timed out."
    action={
      <Button size="sm" variant="outline">
        Try again
      </Button>
    }
  />
</Example>

Written for this page. What must never happen is a failed load wearing the first-use script: "No
renders yet. Generate a render." on a screen where forty renders exist and the request timed out is
a claim the reader then acts on.

## The one-line empties, and the four refusals

Some regions are too small for a title and a body. The registry writes those as a single sentence
with no action, because the control that emptied the list is on screen: "No skills found."
(`skill-menu.tsx:72`), "No generations queued." (`generation-queue.tsx:218`), "No segments yet."
(`tts-composer.tsx:452`), "No artifacts match this type." (`artifact-grid.tsx:224`). The noun is
whatever the reader was looking at, and the sentence closes with a period, per
**Content → Writing mechanics**.

At the other end, four surfaces all say there is nothing here for you and only one of them is an
empty state. The `Boundary` story in `apps/storybook/src/stories/super-ai/EmptyState.stories.tsx`
renders all four side by side with the deciding question in its description: what changes the
outcome. Making something is `empty-state`. Paying is `paywall-message`. Waiting is
`rate-limit-banner`. Rephrasing is `safety-block`. Where nothing the reader can do changes the
outcome, none of the four is the right surface.

## Which empty

- **A region with no data yet.** `empty-state` with an icon, a title, a one-sentence body, and the
  creating action.
- **A filtered or searched region with no matches.** The same component, with the copy switched on
  whether a filter is applied, as `library-shell.tsx:336` does. The action clears the filter.
- **A filtered region whose controls are on screen.** The same, with no action at all.
- **A small region or a picker.** One sentence, no action, no icon.
- **A grid cell.** `size="in-grid"`, which draws a dashed tile and keeps the columns.
- **A collection that failed to load.** `empty-state` with a retry, worded by
  **Content → Error messages**. Nothing ships this today.
- **A single value with no data.** Not an empty state. An em dash placeholder with an `sr-only`
  word, per `track-list.tsx:40` and **Content → Writing mechanics**.
- **Data still in flight.** Not an empty state either. **Patterns → Loading states** owns the wait.
- **One item, one form or one run that failed.** **Content → Error messages**.

## Rules

- Three species, three scripts. Where two could apply, the failure wins: a filtered view whose
  request fails is nothing loaded, because the filter is not the reason.
- First use: the title names what the region holds, the body says how the first one comes to exist,
  and the action creates it when creating is one control away.
- No results: the copy names the filter or the search as the cause and the action undoes it. Never
  a create action, because the reader's data is behind the filter.
- Say the count behind the filter when it is known. `artifact-shell.tsx:276` builds "N of M" for
  exactly this.
- Announce a filtered empty. `artifact-shell.tsx:463` sets `role="status"` so a list emptying is
  not silent.
- A failed load says it failed, in past tense, and offers a retry. Never the first-use script.
- The body is one sentence. The longest shipped description runs two, at `records-shell.tsx:454`.
  Two is the ceiling.
- No illustrations. `empty-state` offers one lucide glyph, rendered `aria-hidden`, and no image
  slot. `ICO-1` and `ICO-2` in `packages/ds-rules/rules/` cover what may be drawn at all.
- Never add a default CTA label. `empty-state.tsx` lines 26 to 31 record why the prop does not
  exist.
- A picker empty is one line and no action, and the noun is whatever the reader searched.
- Loading is not empty. An empty state is a claim about what exists, so hold it until the answer is
  known.
- A block exports an `Empty` story. `apps/docs/scripts/check-contract.mts` asserts it.
- Sentence case, no terminal period on the title, a period on the body, per
  **Content → Writing mechanics**.

This page owns what a zero state says. **Content → Error messages** owns what a failure says, and
the two meet at the failed load, where an error's words sit inside an empty state's frame.
