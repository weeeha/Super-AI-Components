# Resource details

One record's home. The index answered which one; this answers what now. In this registry the frame is
`detail-view-shell`, and its first decision is not layout at all: a record opens as a **popup**, an
**overlay** or a **fullscreen** route, and the three differ in what stays usable behind them. Inside
that frame the record splits into what it **is**, in a left column of fields, and what **happened** to
it, in a right column of channels. Both collapse by measured width rather than by viewport.

## Use it when

- One record has a name, a state, and more facts than a row can carry.
- A reader arrived from **Patterns → Resource index**, or from a link, to do something to that record.
- The record's facts fall into groups a reader works through separately.

Do not use it when the answer is one of these instead:

- **Many records.** **Patterns → Resource index** owns the five views and the narrowing over them.
- **The form is the task.** Once a reader came to fill fields rather than read them,
  **Patterns → Forms & wizards** owns the view.
- **A decision about the record.** `trust-dialog` and `permission-prompt` stop the page to ask, and
  **Patterns → Actions & confirmation** owns what they ask.
- **A conversation about the record.** `chat-shell` and the AI Elements ports are
  **Patterns → AI conversation**'s.
- **A record that failed to load, or holds nothing yet.** **Content → Error messages** and
  **Content → Empty states** own both, and the Empty contract in
  `docs/design-system/concept-model.md` section 4 makes the second one non-optional.

## The detail view, live

One record, three ways of opening it. Switch modes in the demo and watch what happens to the
collection behind the panel.

The three modes are declared in `use-view-mode.tsx` line 62 as `DetailMode`, and the file's own
comment names what each one is for: popup is a centred dialog over a dimmed collection, overlay is a
right-side panel with the collection still live behind it, and fullscreen is a real route, which is
the only mode that owns a URL. The default at line 71 is `overlay`, chosen because it is the only mode
that demonstrates content swapping when a second record is opened.

`detail-view-shell.tsx` line 15 states the restraint that makes one shell serve every entity: it owns
no routing, so the page decides when a mode change means navigating. That is why the shell takes
`mode` as a prop rather than calling `useDetailMode` itself, which is also the single-owner rule
**Patterns → Resource index** records for the collection axis.

Overlay geometry is the part worth reading before overriding it. Line 88 caps the expanded panel at
`w-[min(65vw,100vw_-_540px)]`, and the comment above it shows the arithmetic: the reservation keeps
roughly 300px of live collection at 1280px and 1512px, rising to 432px at 1920px. The stated reason is
that overlay only earns its keep while the board behind stays usable, and the previous
`min-w-[1100px]` did the opposite, leaving about 180px of board on a laptop, which is fullscreen with
extra steps. Below roughly 1180px the file says popup or fullscreen is the honest mode.

### Fields on the left, channels on the right

`detail-view-shell.tsx` line 44 sets `TWO_COLUMN_THRESHOLD` to 720, with the comment that two columns
need roughly 320px each plus gutters. `detail-fields.tsx` line 20 sets its own threshold to 460,
because that is the nested collapse: the pane-level one turns two columns into one, and this one turns
two label and value pairs per row into one. The file measures itself rather than taking a size prop,
so a caller cannot forget to thread it, and it stays a `dl` in both layouts because the collapse is
presentation and the term-to-definition relationship is not.

`detail-tabs.tsx` is one tablist serving both tab levels, level 1 switching panes when the container
is too narrow for two columns and level 2 switching channels inside the conversation. Its line 72
folds a channel's count into the accessible name, `Activity, 3` rather than `Activity`, with the
comment that a coloured badge is invisible to a screen reader. Keyboard behaviour is roving tabindex,
matching the view switcher, so the shell has one keyboard idiom rather than a third.

`asset-detail.tsx` line 17 states the point: the highlighted span is the point, prompts are editable

render them. The design spec section 4 calls it the recipe-card pattern.

`template-detail.tsx` line 32 makes options part of the commit: the primary action emits
`onUseTemplate({ templateId, options })`, so a modal that commits a bare id has only opened a second

## Popup, overlay, or fullscreen

One axis: what does the reader need behind the panel?

- **`popup`** is the centred dialog. `POPUP_HEIGHT` at line 46 is `h-[min(888px,90vh)]`, collapsed
  width `sm:max-w-[1200px]` and expanded `sm:max-w-[1534px]`. The collection dims. Use it when the
  record is the whole task for the next minute and the list behind it is only context.
- **`overlay`** is the right-side panel at `inset-y-2 end-2 h-auto rounded-lg`, line 66. The
  collection stays interactive, which is the reason for the width arithmetic above. Use it when the
  reader is working down a list and opening records one after another.
- **`fullscreen`** is a real route, and the only mode that owns a URL. Use it when the record must be
  linkable, survive a refresh, or hold more than a panel can.

The shell branches on `mode` and renders what it is told, so the three are one component rather than
three. What is not automatic is the routing: `open` is ignored in fullscreen, where the route is the
open state, so a page that offers fullscreen wires navigation itself.

## Key decisions

- **The record says who it is once.** The `header` slot carries the name and the state. No field row
  and no channel repeats it. A pane that has to say the name again wanted to be its own view.
- **Left is what it is, right is what happened.** `attributes` and `conversation` are separate props
  precisely so the split cannot blur. A one-column detail view omits `conversation` rather than
  folding activity into the fields.
- **Collapse on the panel's own width, never the viewport.** Both thresholds are measured against the
  element, 720 for the pane and 460 for the field pairs, because an overlay 640px wide inside a 1920px
  window is narrow whatever the viewport says.
- **The count goes in the name, not only in the badge.** `detail-tabs.tsx` line 72 is the mechanism,
  and the reason is that a badge is invisible to a screen reader.
- **Provenance travels with the asset.** Prompt, model, seed and params render through `stat-readout`
  wherever the asset appears, so two surfaces cannot show two versions of one recipe.
- **Options are configured before commit.** A detail view that commits an id and re-reads options
  afterwards has split one decision in two.
- **The shell owns no routing.** A mode change is the page's to act on, which is the single restraint
  that lets one shell serve every entity.

## Rules

- One record per view, and it states its identity once in `header`. Never repeat the name inside
  `attributes` or a channel.
- Pick the mode by what must stay usable behind the panel: popup dims the collection, overlay keeps it
  live, fullscreen replaces it. Only fullscreen owns a URL, so anything that must be linked or survive
  a refresh takes it.
- Do not widen the overlay past what `detail-view-shell.tsx` line 88 allows. The
  `min(65vw, 100vw - 540px)` cap exists so roughly 300px of collection stays visible on a laptop; a
  fixed minimum wider than that is fullscreen with extra steps.
- Below roughly 1180px, offer popup or fullscreen rather than a three-column overlay. The floor stops
  the collapse from turning into nonsense, and the file says so at its own site.
- Let the components measure themselves. `detail-view-shell` collapses at 720 and `detail-fields` at
  460, both against their own element. Do not thread a viewport breakpoint through either.
- Keep `detail-fields` a `dl` in both layouts. The single-column form is a presentation change, and the
  term-to-definition relationship is not.
- Fold a channel's count into the tab's accessible name, per `detail-tabs.tsx` line 72. A badge alone
  is invisible to a screen reader.
- Use one tablist for both tab levels. A second implementation of the same control drifts, which is
  the reason `detail-tabs.tsx` line 23 gives for serving both.
- Roving tabindex on the tabs, matching the view switcher, so the shell has one keyboard idiom.
- Render provenance through `stat-readout`, not a bespoke grid. Prompt, model, seed, sampler and cost
  travel with the asset, per the Provenance contract in `docs/design-system/concept-model.md`
  section 4, and seed and sampler are copyable because reproducibility depends on them.
- Treat the prompt as material rather than a caption. `asset-detail` highlights a span and feeds it
  into remix; a read-only prompt line hides the one editable thing on the screen.
- Commit the configured object. `onUseTemplate({ templateId, options })` is the shape; emitting a bare
  id makes the host re-read state the component already had.
- Never close a detail view on the way to a related record. `template-detail` leaves `open` untouched
  when `templateId` changes, which is what makes "more like this" structurally unable to dead-end.
- The shell owns no routing. A mode change is the page's to act on, and `open` is ignored in
  fullscreen because the route is the open state.
- Never write `text-muted-foreground` on `bg-muted`, `bg-accent` or `bg-secondary`. `TOK-4`, `TOK-5`
  and `TOK-6` are the rules; the field label in `detail-fields.tsx` line 54 sits on the pane
  background, which is why it can be muted there.
- The record that failed to load is **Content → Error messages**'; the pane holding nothing is
  **Content → Empty states**'; a destructive action on the record is **Patterns → Actions and
  confirmation**'s. This page hands all three off.

## Content notes

The header string is the record's name, not the page's title. `detail-view-shell-demo` writes "Draft
the migration plan", and the demo's `ariaLabel` repeats it so the panel announces the record it holds
rather than "dialog".

A field label names a slot and takes no terminal period: "Owner", "Status", "Due", "Repository",
"Branch". A value is whatever the record holds, formatted once, and `stat-readout` renders an em dash
for a missing one rather than leaving a blank cell, which is why "Guidance" with no value still
occupies a row in `asset-detail-demo`.

Channel labels are nouns naming a place, and the count beside one is a numeral however small:
"Comments" with 2, "Activity" with 3. Sentence case, no terminal period, short enough that a narrow
panel does not have to wrap the tablist.

Prompt text is shown as the reader wrote it, including its own punctuation, because it is the material
being edited. Do not sentence-case a prompt, trim it to a caption length, or paraphrase it in the
detail view.

This page owns how one record's frame is chosen and arranged. **Patterns → Resource index** owns
getting the reader here, **Patterns → Forms & wizards** owns the view once editing is the task,
**Patterns → Status & feedback** owns which surface reports the record's state, and
**Patterns → Actions & confirmation** owns what happens when the reader acts on it.
